### Brand positioning

**Name:** Queup
**Tagline:** Skip the queue. Tap, pay, collect.
**Core promise:** No queuing, no cash, no miscommunication
**Tone:** Direct, British, confident, unpretentious
**Audience:** Two distinct groups — food-loving festival-goers and event attendees (customers); independent food entrepreneurs who want simple tools (vendors)

### Go-to-market strategy

**Phase 1 — Festival summer (months 1–6)**
- Target 2–3 local festivals or food markets as pilot events
- Approach festival organisers directly — offer free platform for the first event
- Each festival gives you 20–40 vendors in one weekend
- Document with photos and testimonials for case studies

**Phase 2 — Vendor-led growth (months 6–12)**
- Vendors who use Queup at festivals recommend it to other vendors
- Home food businesses onboarded via social media (Instagram, TikTok food creators)
- PR in food trade press: Speciality Food Magazine, Street Food News

**Phase 3 — Scale (year 2)**
- Approach food market organisers as channel partners
- Target home food communities (Facebook groups, NCASS members)
- Paid social targeting food entrepreneurs and festival attendees

### Competitive messaging

Queup is NOT:
- A delivery app (no couriers, no delivery fee, no postcode restrictions)
- A restaurant platform (no fixed addresses required)
- A WhatsApp group (structured, paid, tracked)

Queup IS:
- The ordering layer for food that moves
- The tool that replaces cash, queues, and shouted orders
- Built for the informal food economy that delivery apps ignore
